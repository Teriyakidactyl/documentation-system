# 🛠️ Close Work

## 1. Confirm active attention should end

Verify that the Work Object should no longer compete for execution attention.
For completion, check its acceptance conditions. For another outcome, identify
the reason active work intentionally ends.

Do not use close-out to hide unresolved work.

## 2. Assign the disposition

Set `state: closed` and exactly one disposition from the Work Model:
`completed`, `cancelled`, `rejected`, `duplicate`, `superseded`,
`converted`, or `deferred`.

For duplicate, superseded, and converted work, record the surviving target.
For deferred work, record the condition that can make it eligible for
reconsideration.

## 3. Promote surviving current truth

Inspect conclusions produced by the work. Move any rule, architecture,
documentation fact, code behavior, or other current truth into the artifact
whose domain owns it.

A closed Work Object may point to that authority. It must not remain the only
place a future actor can discover a binding rule.

## 4. Preserve required traceability

Retain the minimum facts needed to explain:

- why active attention ended;
- which acceptance conditions were or were not satisfied;
- which Work Objects survive;
- which current authority changed; and
- any evidence needed to understand the disposition.

Do not preserve conversational narration that has no future work or provenance
job.

## 5. Select retention

Use `retention: active` when recent closure, likely reopening, operational
lookup, or nearby related work makes the record useful in its normal
responsibility store.

Use `retention: cold` when the object has a legitimate historical use but no
normal operating role. Move it beneath the corresponding
`.project/Archive/` grouping while preserving its UID and Work ID.

Deletion requires a separate retention reason. It is not the default meaning of
close-out.

## 6. Recompute the execution frontier

Remove the closed object from actionable projections. Re-evaluate downstream
gates whose conditions may now be satisfied or invalidated.

When closure unblocks another Task, update that Task's current state or next
action rather than relying on the closed object's history to imply the change.
