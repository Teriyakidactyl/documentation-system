# 📖 Feedback

Feedback is a retained record of an observation, request, reaction, suggestion,
or critique concerning another subject.

## 1. Subject

Identify what the feedback concerns closely enough that later work can relate to
the same subject without reconstructing conversational context.

## 2. Received content

Preserve the substantive feedback without rewriting it into a Task, Issue, or
solution. Distinguish received content from later interpretation when that
difference matters.

## 3. Context and provenance

Retain source, timing, environment, or other context only when it materially
changes interpretation or later action.

## 4. Relationship to work

Feedback may concern, derive, or motivate work. Record that relationship when
work is created, but do not give the Feedback record Task lifecycle state merely
because action follows.

## 5. Completion

A Feedback record is complete when another reader can recover what was
communicated, what it concerned, and the material context needed to interpret
it.
