# 🛠️ Record Feedback

## 1. Identify the subject

Name the artifact, behavior, proposal, experience, or other subject the feedback
concerns.

## 2. Preserve the feedback

Record the substantive observation, request, reaction, suggestion, or critique.
Do not rewrite it as an executable instruction merely to make it actionable.

## 3. Add material provenance

Record source and context when they change how the feedback should be
interpreted.

## 4. Relate resulting work

When the feedback produces a Task or Plan, preserve the relationship to that
work. Let Work Management own the resulting work state.

## 5. Bind and control the Form

Bind the record to this Form package when Form provenance applies and let
Organizing mint or preserve controlled identity.
