# 📖 Issue

An Issue is a retained record of a recognized condition requiring attention or
disposition.

## 1. Condition

State the condition itself rather than a preferred remedy.

## 2. Evidence

Record the observations or references that establish the condition. Preserve
uncertainty when the condition is not fully characterized.

## 3. Impact

State why the condition matters and what is affected.

## 4. Relationship to work

A Task, Plan, Investigation, or other work may address an Issue. The Issue
remains the record of the condition; Work Management owns executable state.

## 5. Completion

A retained Issue record is sufficient when another reader can identify the
condition, evidence, impact, and any resulting work or disposition.
