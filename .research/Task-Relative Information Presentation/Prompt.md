# Research Prompt: Task-Relative Information Presentation

The fenced block below is the exact rerunnable research payload. Repository
frontmatter and this wrapper are not part of the prompt.

````text
Find independently developed theories, empirical findings, and mature professional practices that distinguish information presentations according to the user's immediate cognitive or operational need. Focus especially on cases where combining two information needs in one representation is known to degrade performance, increase error, or require conflicting presentation structures. Search across information science, cognitive science, human factors, technical communication, decision science, troubleshooting/diagnosis, expert systems, and autonomous-agent research. For each relevant distinction, identify the underlying dimensions, evidence for incompatibility, representation consequences, and whether the distinction plausibly survives for an agent without durable task-specific memory.
````
